# Rules

MadPix is for drawing silly things with friends, and with other players in public drawings
(Play now). To keep it that way:

- **Be kind.** No hateful, harassing, sexual or violent drawings or names.
- **Keep it about the drawing.** Don't draw or write anyone's personal details, like a phone
  number or address.
- **Keep codes for friends.** Share a private drawing's invite code with people you know.
  Anyone can join a public drawing.

Anyone in a drawing can report it from the drawing's screen. We look at every report. Drawings
that break these rules are deleted, and players who break them are removed. A reported public
drawing stops being offered to new players right away.

You can also **block** another artist from a drawing's screen. You'll never be put in a public
drawing or a rematch with them again. They aren't told.
