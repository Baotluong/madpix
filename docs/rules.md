# Rules

MadPix is for drawing silly things with friends. To keep it that way:

- **Be kind.** No hateful, harassing, sexual or violent drawings or names.
- **Keep it about the drawing.** Don't draw or write anyone's personal details, like a phone
  number or address.
- **Only invite people you know.** Invite codes are for your friends.

Anyone in a drawing can report it from the drawing's screen. We look at every report. Drawings
that break these rules are deleted, and players who break them are removed.
